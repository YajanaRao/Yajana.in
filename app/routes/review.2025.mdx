### Wealth

In terms of Investment I have been passive this year. Active stock picking requires constant attention which I don't want to give so I have been increasing the mutual fund allocation this year. Given the consolidation phase of Nifty 50 and smallcap stocks, it has been a pleasure to accumulate units.

New Entrants to portfolio are Gold, Silver and Helios Smallcap fund.

Going forward I want to cutdown on some of the stocks that are not performing well and keep investing in mutual funds. I will keep an eye out for Helios Smallcap fund. As it is a new to the market a little bit of due diligence is required. I am thinking of not increasing the position in nifty 50 index fund once Parag Parikh Large Cap Fund is live. I have not made my mind up in investing into NFO.

I am also thinking of restructuring my portfolio from having a lot of money in Savings account into arbitrage fund and liquid funds. Having money in savings account gives me confidence whereas not the smartest thing to do considering tax implications.

Even though goal is to be financially free, I am not thinking of retiring yet. Feels like I am just begining the exploration of what is possible to me
