### Meditation

I started the year with 4 days silent retreat. I have been doing 30 minutes of meditation of most days. I have tried to be mindful throught the day which is still a long way to go. Long walks, time before going to sleep and long conversations have been more mindful then before. The Krishnamurti's Notebook is also quite effective in taking me to meditatives states when I read it during right times. I would like to reduce screen time so that I can spend more time into those activities which can lead to mindful states.

### Wealth
Interms of Investment I have been passive this year. Active stock picking requires constant attention which I don't want to give so I have been increasing the mutual fund allocation this year. Given the consolidation phase of Nifty 50 and smallcap stocks, it has been a pleasure to accumulate units. 

New Entrants to portfolio are Gold, Silver and Helios Smallcap fund. 


Going forward I want to cutdown on some of the stocks that are not performing well and keep investing in mutual funds. I will keep an eye out for Helios Smallcap fund. As it is a new to the market a little bit of due deligence is required. I am thinking of not increasing the position in nifty 50 index fund once Parag Parikh Large Cap Fund is live. I have not made my mind up in investing into NFO.

I am also thinking of restructing my portfolio from having a lot of money in idfc first savings account into arbitrage fund and liquid funds. Having money in savings account gives me confidence where as not the smartest thing to do considering tax implications.
