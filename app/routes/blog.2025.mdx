Started the year with a 5 days silent meditation retreat by Art of Living. This is the first time attending a advanced meditation program by Art of Living. I could not have asked for a better start to the year. It was fun.

After the meditation retreat I did work from office for a week as our CEO was visiting Bangalore office. had dinner In [1BK](https://share.google/8mQ0IIKQvwLQQHdYp), We played badmitton and had a long 1 on 1 discussion. Next weekend I visited Hubli for an event by my college to welcome next batch of students. Played badmitton, cricket and returned for the work for Monday.

Next I travelled for one of my collegue's farewell in April. During the visted I attended Ecstacy of Enlightenment by Sadhguru. This was the first time I got an opportunity to See Sadhguru live. I and my friend enrolled into the course. We booked the cheapest seating available. Still we could see Sadhguru from very close. There were different meditations as well. It was fun.

### Finance
Interms of Investment I have been passive this year. Active stock picking requires constant attention which I don't want to give so I have been increasing the mutual fund allocation this year. Given the consolidation phase of Nifty 50 and smallcap stocks, it has been a pleasure to accumulate units. 

New Entrants to portfolio are Gold, Silver and Helios Smallcap fund. 

- Stock portfolio outperformed Nifty by 4.85x
- MF portfolio outperformed Nifty by 1.29x
- Stock portfolio delivered 3.76x higher returns than MF portfolio
